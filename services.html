Our Services:

We offer a number of services to make sure your furry friend can live their best life!
From basic grooming to advanced training, we have you covered!

Grooming

We offer all types of grooming services. From haircuts and baths to trims and brushings, you name it, we do it! Mobile service available depending on location and need.

Boarding

We offer boarding and sitting services for all pups, regardless of size or dietary needs. Health needs must be specified when booking appointments. Activities include two 30 minute walks everyday and access to various toys. Charges are based on duration of stay and boarding requirements.

Training

Our training programs specialize in communication, socialization, obedience, leash manners, heeling, staying, sitting, and lying down. One-on-One and group training sessions are offered depending on availability and requirements.
